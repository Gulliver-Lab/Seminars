How to use

Speakers

Search or sort the list of people we want to present, or presented in the past. Click a row to edit it, or choose New Speaker to add someone. Please add as much information (email, affiliation, group website) as possible.

Calendar

Click a week to add or update its talk. Choose a speaker, status, status date, organizer, title, and abstract, then save. Use No talk to record a reason, or Remove talk to delete the entry.
